Three routes

Pick the one that fits the way you own cars.

There is no single best answer. It depends on how long you keep a car, how much cash you want to put down, and whether the vehicle qualifies for a manufacturer program.

01 · Manufacturer

GM Financial

Often the sharpest route on a new or certified Cadillac when the model carries a promotional rate. We check the current programs before you commit.

02 · Local lender

Bank or credit union

A strong down payment or a shorter term can beat dealer financing. Ed works with any lender you choose and does not mark up the rate.

03 · Lower payment

Lease

Lower monthly cost and a fixed term, without building equity. It makes sense if you like a new car every few years and want the number to stay predictable.

What shapes the number

Five things move a payment more than the sticker.

  • Credit history. The score and the depth of the file decide the tier.
  • Down payment and trade. Either one lowers what you finance.
  • Term length. A longer term lowers the payment and raises the interest.
  • Vehicle age and mileage. Pre-owned rates often differ from new.
  • Taxes and fees. They are part of the amount financed, not a surprise at signing.
