## Trading In Your Vehicle

A trade-in is one of the simplest ways to reduce what you owe on a new purchase.
Two numbers matter: what your car is worth, and how much of that goes toward your
new Cadillac.

### How Trade-In Value Is Determined

- Year, make, model, trim, and mileage
- Overall condition, interior and exterior
- Service history and whether records are complete
- Current market demand and comparable sales
- Any outstanding balance or lien on the vehicle

### Reducing Loan Balance

If you still owe money on the vehicle you are trading, the difference between the
payoff amount and the trade-in value is either:

- **Negative equity** — rolled into the new loan, increasing what you finance
- **Positive equity** — coming back to you as a down payment or cheque

### Before You Come In

- Gather service records and the title or registration
- Note the VIN and confirm the odometer reading
- Remove personal belongings and factory reset any electronics

## Get an Appraisal

An in-person appraisal gives you a firm number. Bring the vehicle, the title, and
any service records.
