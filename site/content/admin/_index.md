Every card below says how a change made there reaches the public site:
**Live** means it is stored in the database and visible on the next page
load. **Next sync + deploy** means it is stored safely now and applied to
the site’s files by the inventory sync, then published by the next
deploy. **Needs a deploy** means it commits to the repository and waits for
a build.
