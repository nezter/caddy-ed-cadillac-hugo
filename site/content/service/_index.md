## Factory-Trained Service

Keeping a Cadillac in factory condition protects the warranty and the resale
value. Caddy Ed arranges factory-trained service for vehicles purchased here, and
can advise on independent options if the vehicle is no longer under warranty.

### Routine Maintenance

- Oil and filter changes at the manufacturer interval
- Tire rotation, balancing, and alignment
- Brake inspection and pad replacement
- Fluid checks and replacement schedules

### Diagnostics and Repair

- Warning light diagnosis
- Suspension and steering work
- Climate control and electrical faults
- Scheduled and unscheduled repair work

### Collision

Body and paint work, panel replacement, and post-collision calibration. Collision
work is especially important on modern Cadillacs, which rely on cameras and radar
systems that need recalibration after any repair.
