## Overview

This policy explains what information is collected when you use this site, why it
is collected, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

- **Information you provide** — name, email, phone number, and messages submitted
  through contact, test-drive, or communication-preference forms
- **Vehicle and trade-in details** — VIN, mileage, and condition information you
  provide during a trade appraisal
- **Technical data** — browser type, device type, and pages visited, collected in
  aggregate

## How We Use It

- To respond to enquiries and schedule appointments
- To send information you have explicitly requested
- To improve the site and the experience of visiting it
- To meet legal and tax record-keeping obligations

## Your Choices

- Opt out of marketing communication at any time using the communication
  preferences page
- Request a copy of the personal information held about you
- Request deletion of that information, subject to legal retention requirements

## Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected
for, or as required by law. Access is limited to staff who need it.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the [contact page](/contact/).
